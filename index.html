The science

Built on how people actually learn.

Range buckets don't teach skills — deliberate practice does. Tee Time Trainer bakes four principles from motor-learning research into every session.

01

Clear, specific goals.

Every rep has a target, a score, and a finish line — because vague practice produces vague results.

→
02

Focus in small doses.

Sessions are built in short focused blocks, not one-hour bucket dumps. Your brain keeps up. Your form holds.

→
03

Feedback after every shot.

Instant, honest signal — not hunches from the last nine holes you played a week ago.

→
04

Stress, then rest.

Pressure reps interleaved with recovery — the pattern elite coaches use to move skills from range to course.

→
